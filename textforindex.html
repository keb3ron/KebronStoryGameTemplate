The Missing Watch

Welcome to The Missing Watch. Before you start investigating, tell me a little about yourself, detective.

It’s Grandpa’s 80th birthday. The lights go out during dinner. When they come back on, his 100-year-old gold watch is gone. Three people were close enough to take it. You have until midnight to find out who.

Marta, the cook. Been with the family 20 years. In and out of the kitchen all night.

Sam, the gardener. Dropped off flowers for the party. Standing near the dessert table when the lights went out.

Dave, the cousin. Showed up late. Sat closest to Grandpa. Short on money lately.
